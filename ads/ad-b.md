# Ad B: "Koupeno. A co dál?" (fear of what comes after)

**Placement:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram), mobile.
**Creatives:**
- Feed 4:5, 1080 × 1350: [`ad-b-feed-4x5.png`](ad-b-feed-4x5.png)
- Story 9:16, 1080 × 1920: [`ad-b-story-9x16.png`](ad-b-story-9x16.png)

**Who it's for:** people who are about to buy, or just bought, their first ETF and are unsure what happens next: drops, dividends, taxes.
**Landing:** hero variant B (`?v=b`). The hero headline must repeat the ad's promise.

| Description | Poklesy, dividendy, daně – přehledně |
| CTA button | Další informace (Learn more) |

**Source of the numbers:** SPY monthly adjusted closes (Alpha Vantage). Peak Oct 2007 → low Feb 2009 −51 %, back above the previous peak in March 2012. Month-end prices; the intra-month drop was deeper.

## Why it should work

- It speaks to a fear almost no ETF comparison addresses: what happens *after* the first purchase.
- It uses a real historical fact instead of a promise.
- It's a different motivation from Ad A, so the two ads test which pain point brings cheaper leads (A/B hypothesis 1).

## Risk

- Starting with "−51 %" can scare people away instead of drawing them in. The copy follows it immediately with the recovery and keeps the past-performance warning.
