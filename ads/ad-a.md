# Ad A: "Které ETF si koupit jako první?" (choice overload)

**Placement:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram), mobile.
**Creatives:**
- Feed 4:5, 1080 × 1350: [`ad-a-feed-4x5.png`](ad-a-feed-4x5.png)
- Story 9:16, 1080 × 1920: [`ad-a-story-9x16.png`](ad-a-story-9x16.png)

**Who it's for:** Czech retail investors who have heard of ETFs but don't know which one to start with.
**Landing:** hero variant A (default). The hero headline repeats the ad headline word for word.

## Copy (Czech)

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| Primary text | Chcete začít s ETF, ale nevíte, které vybrat? Odpovězte na 2 otázky a za 20 vteřin uvidíte fond, který se k vám hodí – i s poplatky a tím, jak ho koupit z Česka. Zdarma, bez registrace. |
| Headline | Které ETF si koupit jako první? |
| Description | 2 otázky · 20 vteřin · zdarma |
| CTA button | Další informace (Learn more) |

## Image

- **Scene:** a stressed man at a laptop late in the evening, lit by the screen, holding his head. Generated in Nano Banana without any text.
- **Added in code:**
  - the headline
  - seven coloured ETF chips, each with ticker + what it tracks: VWCE (celý svět), VOO, SPY, CSPX, VUAA (all S&P 500), IWDA and EUNL (both MSCI World)
  - three question marks
  - "2 otázky · 20 vteřin · zdarma"
  - a green CTA "Zjistit, které se hodí mně →"
- **Why the chips:** eight names, but really only three kinds of fund. That's the confusion the page clears up.
- **Font:** Bricolage Grotesque.
- **Q avoided:** QQQ was replaced by EUNL because the font's Q is hard to read at large sizes.

## Why it should work

- It names a concrete moment: standing in front of thousands of ETFs with no idea where to start.
- It promises a fast, concrete answer (2 questions, 20 seconds), not "learn about investing".
- The page delivers that answer before asking for anything.

## Before launch

- Check Meta's current rules for financial-services ads in Czechia (advertiser verification, special ad category). Not verified here.
- The ad doesn't recommend a product or promise returns; the page carries the disclaimer.
